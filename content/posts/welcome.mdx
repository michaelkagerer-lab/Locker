Every website needs a first post.

This one is mine. I've wanted a place to write for a while — not a social feed, not a newsletter, not a carefully curated portfolio. Just a place where I can put things down and let them exist. A room of my own on the internet, so to speak.

## What This Is

This site is three things:

1. **A blog.** I write when something is worth writing about. That might be a project I'm working on, something I read that changed how I think, or just an observation I can't stop turning over in my head.

2. **A gallery.** Photography is how I slow down. I shoot mostly landscapes, sometimes cities at night. The gallery is where I keep the ones I want to remember.

3. **An archive.** I like the idea that writing things down makes them a little more real, a little more permanent. This site is a way of saying: *this happened, I thought about it, here's what I came up with.*

## On Simplicity

I deliberately kept the design minimal. Not because minimal is fashionable (though it is), but because I wanted the content to be the thing. Too many personal sites feel like they're showing off their CSS. I'd rather the writing and the photos carry the weight.

If you're reading this via RSS — thank you. RSS is one of those old web technologies that deserves to survive, and it's my favorite way to follow people I find interesting. There's a feed link in the footer if you want to subscribe.
